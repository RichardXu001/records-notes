About this site

About The Records Bench

The Records Bench is an independent reference site about laboratory documentation: the receiving record written at the moment of delivery, the batch documents that travel with a container, and the storage log that sits beside the unit. It is written from the outside, for people who fill in those pages rather than for anyone who sells the material on them.

What This Site Is

Four working notes, each about a document a laboratory already keeps. Every note takes the same approach: name the fields that carry weight, describe what is usually missing from them, and explain why the order of the page changes how the page gets used. The scope is deliberately narrow. No product pages, no catalogue, no side-by-side tables, and no material described as better than another.

Who Writes It

The notes are signed R. Chen, lab documentation. That is a pen name used for this site only, chosen so the writing stands on the strength of the argument instead of on a name. There is no institutional affiliation to imply, no laboratory to name, and no credential claimed beyond having filled in these forms and read other people's versions of them.

What This Site Is Not

It is not an official site, and it is not the site of any manufacturer, distributor, or laboratory supplier. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, sponsored by, or operated by a supplier. No brand is promoted here, and no page collects enquiries on behalf of anyone. Where a note links to another site, the link points at a page that illustrates a point being made in the text; it is a reference, not a recommendation.

How the Notes Are Written

Supplier documentation practices differ widely, and a good deal of what a customer receives is thinner than the page it arrives on. Comparing a few published document sets is the quickest way to see the range: an example of a batch record page usually shows the same handful of elements — an identifier, a date, a storage condition — arranged in a supplier's own style. The notes here describe the elements rather than any one arrangement, because the arrangement is the part that changes.

Disclaimer

Everything on this site is general commentary on documentation practice. Nothing here is a statement about a specific product, batch, supplier, or shipment, and nothing here should be read as one. The site publishes no analytical results, no specifications, no conclusions about the suitability of any material, and no advice on how a particular laboratory should run its records.

Materials handled in laboratory work are for laboratory research use only, and the person handling them is responsible for their own procedures and their own records. Descriptions of forms and fields are offered as examples, not as requirements, and no statement on this site should be relied on in place of an organisation's own procedures, its quality documents, or the instructions supplied with a material.

This site loads no external stylesheets, no scripts, and no analytics. Every page is a single file, which is also why there is no cookie banner to dismiss.
